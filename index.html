The problem

Latam Scalability

Latin America and Caribbean is a very fragmented region, with 640 million people spread in 33 attractive but small markets, each one of them with their own cultural, political and financial structure.

To be successful in their expansion, startups spend huge amount of time and resources to create complex structures and build strong local networks, facing a risk that is not part of their core competences.

WeBoost, a venture capital firm dedicated solely to find, accelerate & scale local Latin American startups through all the region.
